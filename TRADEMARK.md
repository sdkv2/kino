## Forks

Forking is welcome — that is the point of MIT. But a fork you distribute or operate needs its
own name and its own branding. State the relationship factually ("a fork of kino") rather than
carrying the mark forward.

## Questions

Ask before you ship: open an issue at
[github.com/sdkv2/kino/issues](https://github.com/sdkv2/kino/issues). Permission for uses not
covered here is usually easy to get in writing, and cheaper than a rename.
